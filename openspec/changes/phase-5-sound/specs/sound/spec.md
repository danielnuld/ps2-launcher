## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sound set
The launcher SHALL ship 5 original synthesized sounds, generated by `tools/sfx.py` in the cybercore Y2K style the user
approved on the PC:
- `splash`: about 4.4 s, cues at 0, 0.5, 1.2-1.6 and 2.4 s of the splash timeline.
- `move`, `edge`, `confirm`, `panel`.

#### Scenario: Regenerate
- **WHEN** `python tools/sfx.py` runs
- **THEN** it writes `sfx/*.wav` (48 kHz mono, 16-bit, normalized to -1 dBFS, silent tails trimmed) and its
  selftest passes

### Requirement: Playback
The sounds SHALL play as SPU2 ADPCM on free voices through audsrv, without blocking the render thread for more than
one RPC per event:
- ← / →: `move`, or `edge` at the end of the row.
- X: `confirm`.
- SELECT: `panel`.

#### Scenario: Fast navigation
- **WHEN** the user presses → several times quickly
- **THEN** each press plays its own `move` on a free voice (overlapping, not cut off), and the frame gate still holds

### Requirement: Splash sync
The splash timeline and the `splash` sound SHALL start on the same frame, after the black hold and once audio is
ready. If audio is not ready 6 s after the hold, the splash starts silent.

#### Scenario: Boot
- **WHEN** the ELF boots on the console
- **THEN** the orb's hit sound lands on the orb's appearance (1.6 s into the timeline)
